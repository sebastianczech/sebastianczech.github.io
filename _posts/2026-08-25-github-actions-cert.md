I'd like to share another achievement – today I passed the [GitHub Actions (GH-200)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/github-actions/?practice-assessment-type=certification) exam, confirmed by [my credential](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/users/sebastianczech-4206/credentials/54aca840adf60ac0).

The exam targets DevOps engineers, developers, and IT professionals with intermediate experience in GitHub Actions – authoring workflows, automating processes, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines.

The exam itself takes 100 minutes, is proctored, and requires a score of at least 700 to pass.

What helped the most, though, was hands-on practice: writing my own workflows, debugging the ones that broke, and building reusable workflows shared across several repositories.
